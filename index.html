THE TRADING JOURNAL PRO

Enterprise Mobile Application for Stock Market Traders

📌 Purpose of Application

THE TRADING JOURNAL PRO is a professional Android mobile application built for equity, stock market, and F&O traders to systematically log, analyze, and optimize their daily trading performance.

Why Google User Authentication is Required:

  • User Account Authentication: Google Sign-In (OAuth) is used strictly to authenticate users and create their private, secure trading profile.
  • Encrypted Trade Syncing: Securely sync trade journal entries, buy/sell prices, notes, and strategy logs across user devices.
  • Automated Broker Statement Import: Import and parse broker CSV trade statements (Zerodha, Dhan, Groww, AngelOne, Upstox, Kotak) to generate automated win-rate analytics, risk-to-reward metrics, and drawdown reports.
  • Privacy & AES-256 Protection: Ensure that all user trading data and journal entries are strictly encrypted with AES-256 and accessible only by the authenticated account holder. We do NOT share or sell user trading data to any third party.

🛡️ Key Capabilities & Features

  • Automated Broker CSV Import: One-tap trade parsing for major stock broker statements.
  • Analytics & Metrics: Track daily P&L, Win Rate %, Profit Factor, Expectancy, and Drawdown curves.
  • Smart Money & Position Sizing Calculators: Built-in financial tools to prevent over-leveraging and manage capital risk.
  • Cloud Backup & Data Export: Full control to export or backup trade journals anytime.
