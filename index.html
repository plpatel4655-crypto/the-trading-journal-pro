📌 Purpose of THE TRADING JOURNAL PRO

THE TRADING JOURNAL PRO is an advanced Android mobile application built for equity, stock market, and F&O traders to systematically log, analyze, and optimize their trading performance.

Why User Authentication & Data Access is Needed:

  • Secure User Profile: Google OAuth is used to authenticate users and securely create their private trading journal profile.
  • Encrypted Journaling: Securely store trade logs, entry/exit prices, position sizing, and risk-to-reward ratios.
  • Automated Broker Analytics: Import and parse broker CSV trade statements (Zerodha, Dhan, Groww, AngelOne, Upstox) to calculate win-rate percentage, drawdown metrics, and profit factor.
  • Data Protection: Ensure user trade journals are strictly encrypted with AES-256 and accessible only by the authenticated user account.

🛡️ Key Features & Capabilities

  • Automated Broker CSV Import: Instant parsing for Zerodha, Dhan, Groww, AngelOne, Upstox, and Kotak statements.
  • Advanced Analytics Dashboard: Win-rate calculations, profit factor metrics, drawdown charts, and daily P&L tracking.
  • AES-256 Encryption: Your trade entries and backups are protected with enterprise-grade encryption.
  • Smart Money & Risk Tools: Calculator modules for risk management and position sizing.
